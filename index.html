High concept · PC & web browser

Be the mad scientist of futuristic cooking.

A goofy precision-cooking simulator in a futuristic kitchen.

Cook food in first-person view like a mad scientist, and watch what it does to your test subjects. Every recipe is a chain of steps, each with its own gameplay. The better you cook each step, the stronger the goofy effect on the subject who eats the dish: they glow, float, change color or grow a giant head.

Design pillars
Precision

Every step is measured

Weight, temperature, time, angle

Each step has a real target in real units (g, mL, K, °C, rpm, mm). Precision is scored and drives the outcome.

Mad science

Futuristic tools & elements

Cryo, plasma, quantum

Familiar gestures with impossible equipment: plasma butter, cryo-chives, helium flour, a centrifuge whisk. New gear unlocks new kinds of effects.

Consequences

See the result on the subject

Dosage → effect strength

Good dosing gives the requested effect. Sloppy dosing gives side effects. Bad dosing backfires: the subject turns green or shrinks. The payoff is visual and immediate.

Immersion

Realistic, step-by-step

Slice to cut, turn to stir

First-person bench with two hands. Mouse gestures copy the real motion: drag to slice, circle to stir, hold to pour, scroll to set heat.

Player promise

“I nailed 37.0 °C and the monkey's head doubled in size. Let's see what 40 does.” Curiosity first, then mastery.

Learning by playing

Units, conversions, Maillard reaction, emulsions and phase changes are taught through the mechanics, not through text screens.

No harm to test subjects

Subjects are volunteers on the lab's payroll. Effects are temporary, harmless and silly. Every subject leaves the bench happy.

Targets & risks

  • All recipes are data-driven (JSON) so designers can tune without code

3D style principles

Shape language

Chunky, rounded primitives with toy proportions: big heads, short limbs, readable silhouettes at thumbnail size. Effects push those proportions (head ×1.5, body ×1.3) without breaking the rig.

Materials

Two families. The lab is physically based: brushed steel, clear glass, emissive reagents. Subjects and food are cel-shaded (toon ramp) so they pop against the metal.

Lighting

Soft key light from the top right, cool fill from the floor. Glowing ingredients carry their own point light onto the subject, so a glow effect lights up the bench.

Cameras

In-game: fixed first-person over the bench. Layout editor: orbit camera over the kitchen. Codex and results: turntable around the subject.

Rules for subjects

Expressive faces, never in pain. A backfire reads as dizzy (X eyes) or sickly green, not hurt. Every effect wears off after the shift.
